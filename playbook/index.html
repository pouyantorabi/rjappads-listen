The method

Great campaigns are a loop, not a launch.

01 · PLANPick one goalBe remembered, or get clicked. The goal picks your format, your market and your creative.
02 · LAUNCHStart focusedOne market, one budget you can hold steady, and two to four creatives to compare.
03 · MEASURERead the reportAds Manager reports every creative separately. Find the winner, retire the loser.
04 · REFINEChange one thingSwap the weakest creative for a new one. One change at a time, so you know what moved.
